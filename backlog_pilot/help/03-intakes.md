# Intakes and change control

An approved request lives on as an intake: its source text, summary, assumptions, and open
questions, with every version of each.

## Assumptions and questions

Anyone signed in can edit, answer, remove, restore, or add an assumption or question. Every
change is a new version recording what changed, who changed it, when, and why. The AI's
original wording stays as version 1.

## When a change may affect the backlog

Some changes could make the drafted stories wrong:

- any change a person makes to an assumption;
- a question being answered (or its answer changing).

Adding, rewording, or removing a question is captured but not flagged. When there are flagged
changes, the intake shows **may affect the backlog**, and an approver decides:

- **Re-draft with updates:** the AI drafts again from the source and the current notes (live
  AI mode; the offline demo explains this); the old items are kept as *Superseded*, never
  overwritten.
- **No backlog change needed:** the decision is logged with who made it.
