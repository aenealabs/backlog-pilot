# Backlog

The Backlog page shows every epic, feature, and story, with where each came from.

## Reading an item

- **AI-drafted** items came from an approved draft; the **Intake** chip opens the request.
- **Reworded** (hover to see) means the reviewer changed the AI's title; the AI's wording is
  kept.
- **Traced / AI inference / Quote not found** shows whether a story's quotes are in the
  stakeholder's input.
- A highlighted persona chip means the persona is not in the persona library.
- **Related** chips are existing items the reviewer linked during review.

## Jira and Azure DevOps

When a tracker is configured, approved items are pushed parents first (epic, feature, story)
with acceptance criteria, WSJF, product, and a link back. If a push fails, the approval
stands, the item shows **Sync pending** with the reason, and an approver can retry. Backlog
Pilot stays the record of intakes and decisions.
